# Recipe Design Spec — Steve's Health Protocol

*A guide for creating recipes that fit my diet and long-term goals. It codifies the
protocol already built into my tracking app — it is not medical advice, it's my
established plan written down so a recipe (or a recipe-generating AI) can hit it.*

---

## 0. The core principle — read this first

**Every recipe is a building block of a whole day, not a complete meal-in-one.**

A recipe does **not** have to balance all my macros by itself. It just has to be
*useful to the whole day* and never break a hard constraint. Specifically:

- It does **not** need to contain resistant starch. If it does, that's a bonus.
- It does **not** need to hit some perfect protein/carb/fat ratio on its own.
- It **does** need to avoid the "never" list, and it *should* lean toward the
  levers that matter (protein density, low saturated fat, good fats).

Think of each recipe as filling a **slot** in the day (a protein anchor, a
cook-and-cool starch side, a veg-and-fiber component, a snack, a post-workout
refuel) — and the day as a whole is where balance happens.

---

## 1. Who I am / what I'm optimizing for

- 43-year-old man, resistance-training 4–5×/week.
- **Primary goal: body recomposition** — build muscle and lose fat at the same time.
  This runs at maintenance or a *mild* surplus, **never an aggressive deficit.**
- Managing, per an established medical protocol, in rough priority order:
  1. **Liver — NASH (early, reversible fatty-liver).** Genetic predisposition
     (PNPLA3 + MBOAT7). This is the top dietary priority.
  2. **Cholesterol** — I'm on a statin.
  3. **Reflux (GERD)** — I'm on a PPI (pantoprazole).
  4. **No gallbladder** (cholecystectomy) — fat is handled, but large single-meal
     fat loads are uncomfortable, so fat should be spread out and moderate per dish.
- **No alcohol, ever.**

---

## 2. The day each recipe is fitting into

Macros are split across ~5–6 eating occasions (breakfast, mid-morning, lunch,
pre-gym, dinner, post-gym / snacks).

| Target | Training day | Rest day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calories | ~2280 | ~2000 | maintenance / mild surplus for recomp |
| Protein | ~205 g | ~200 g | **the hard one — bias every recipe toward it** |
| Carbs | ~172 g | ~140 g | quality carbs; flexible |
| Fat | ~75 g | ~65 g | mostly unsaturated |
| Resistant starch | 20 g → ramping to 40 g | same | **day-level goal, per-recipe bonus** |
| Fiber | ~35 g | same | soluble especially welcome |
| Saturated fat | **keep under ~15 g** | same | minimize — near a hard cap |

**Weekly, across all recipes:**
- 🐟 **Fatty fish ≥ 3 meals/week** (salmon, sardines, mackerel, trout, tuna).
- 🥩 **Red meat ≤ 2 meals/week**, lean cuts only.
- 🍵 Green tea daily; walnuts + ground flax most days (weave in when natural).

---

## 3. Hard constraints — a recipe must NEVER

- **No grapefruit or grapefruit juice** (statin / CYP3A4 interaction).
- **No alcohol** (including as a cooking reduction).
- **No deep-frying or battered-fried food** (reflux + liver).
- **No butter, coconut oil, cream, cream cheese, or full-fat dairy as a fat source;**
  minimize saturated fat generally. (Tiny amounts of parmesan/feta as *seasoning*
  are fine.)
- **No reflux triggers** as core ingredients: tomatoes & tomato sauce, citrus
  (orange/lemon/lime/grapefruit), spicy/chili/hot sauce, chocolate,
  mint/peppermint, **raw** garlic, **raw** onion, heavy cream sauces.
- **No cured/processed meats** as a staple (bacon, sausage, hot dogs, most deli
  cured meats — nitrates + sodium + saturated fat). Occasional, lean, low-sodium
  deli turkey/chicken is the exception.
- **Red meat only if lean** (sirloin, 90 %+ lean) — and it spends one of my ≤2
  weekly red-meat slots, so don't build lots of recipes around it.
- **No fat bombs.** Because I have no gallbladder, keep fat moderate per serving
  (roughly ≤ ~20 g fat in a single dish) and unsaturated.

---

## 4. Bias every recipe toward these (the levers that matter)

In rough priority:

1. **Protein-forward.** Getting to ~200 g/day is the hardest target and the recomp
   lever. Most recipes should carry meaningful protein — aim for dishes that are
   *protein-dense for their calories* (a rough goal: **≥ ~8–10 g protein per 100
   kcal**; e.g. a ~400-kcal main carrying 30–40 g protein).
2. **Low saturated fat.** A single serving should take only a small bite of the
   ~15 g/day cap (aim ≤ ~4 g sat per serving where possible).
3. **Good fats, in moderation.** Extra-virgin olive oil (my primary fat), avocado,
   nuts/seeds, oily fish. Not saturated fats. Moderate load per dish.
4. **Fiber** — especially soluble (oats, legumes, chia, flax, barley).

---

## 5. Bonuses — include when they fit naturally (never forced)

- ❄ / 🌾 **Resistant starch** (see §7 for how).
- 🐟 **Omega-3s** — oily fish; walnuts and ground flax (plant ALA).
- 🫘 **Soluble fiber** — oats, legumes, barley, chia.
- 🥬 **Vegetables**, especially leafy greens and cruciferous.
- 🥛 **Fermented foods** — kefir, Greek yogurt (gut/butyrate synergy with RS).
- 🫚 **Ginger** — reflux-friendly and flavorful.

---

## 6. Preferred ingredient palette

**Proteins:** white fish (cod, tilapia), oily fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel,
trout, tuna), skinless chicken/turkey breast, lean ground turkey/chicken, egg
whites + whole eggs (moderate yolks), Greek yogurt, low-fat cottage cheese, kefir,
whey/casein, tofu/tempeh/edamame, lean sirloin (sparingly).

**Fats (unsaturated):** extra-virgin olive oil (primary), avocado & avocado oil,
walnuts, almonds, pistachios, chia, ground flax, no-sugar nut butters, tahini,
hemp & pumpkin seeds.

**Carbs / starch:** oats (raw for RS), quinoa, barley, brown & white rice (cooled
for RS), potatoes & sweet potatoes (cooled for RS), legumes (chickpeas, cannellini,
black/kidney/pinto beans, lentils), sourdough / sprouted (Ezekiel) / whole-grain
bread, fruit (berries preferred — high fiber, gentle fructose load).

**Vegetables:** leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, brussels sprouts, bok choy,
kale, asparagus, zucchini, bell peppers, cucumber, carrots, mushrooms, green beans.

**Reflux-safe flavor toolkit** (this is where creativity lives, since so many
punchy flavors are out):
- Fresh & dried herbs — basil, oregano, parsley, cilantro, dill, thyme, rosemary.
- Ginger; **cooked/caramelized** onion & garlic (never raw); toasted sesame.
- Vinegars in moderation (balsamic, rice, red-wine), low-sodium soy sauce, mustard.
- Roasted red pepper, mushrooms, small amounts of parmesan, nutritional yeast for
  umami depth without tomato/citrus/chili.

---

## 7. How to add resistant starch (when a recipe has starch)

- **RS3 — retrograded (cook, then cool):** cook rice / potato / legumes / pasta,
  refrigerate **≥ 4 h (overnight best)**, then serve **cold or gently reheated —
  never re-boiled** (boiling destroys it). Perfect for grain/potato/bean salads,
  cold noodle bowls, make-ahead lunches.
- **RS2 — raw / ungelatinized:** raw rolled oats (overnight oats, no-bake bites),
  green-tipped banana, Bob's unmodified potato starch (**cold-mix only, never
  heated** — stir into yogurt/smoothies).

If a recipe includes a starch and can be made cook-and-cool, prefer that framing.
If it can't, that's fine — no RS is not a failure.

---

## 8. Prep rules

- **No frying.** Bake, roast, steam, poach, grill, or light sauté in olive oil.
- **Creaminess without cream:** blend Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, silken tofu,
  white beans, or tahini instead of cream/butter/cheese sauces.
- **Cook-and-cool** for any RS3 dish (see §7).
- **Dinner should finish ≥ 3 h before lying down** — dinner recipes can lean a
  little lighter and earlier-friendly.

---

## 9. What a finished recipe should specify

For each recipe, state:

1. **Name**, the **meal slot(s)** it suits (breakfast / mid-morning / lunch /
   pre-gym / dinner / post-gym / snack) and **day-type** (training / rest / either),
   and **number of servings**.
2. **Ingredients with gram weights** (I log by weight, so grams matter).
3. **Per-serving macros:** calories, protein, carbs, fat, **saturated fat**, fiber,
   and **estimated resistant starch**.
4. **Protocol levers it hits** — e.g. 🐟 omega-3 fish · ❄ RS3 (cooled) ·
   🌾 RS2 (raw) · high soluble fiber · contains walnuts/flax · fermented.
5. **Flags to note** — e.g. "lean red meat — uses a weekly slot," or "higher fat,
   pair with lighter meals today."
6. **Prep notes** — especially cook-and-cool timing and make-ahead steps.

---

## 10. Final self-check (run before calling a recipe done)

- [ ] Protein-forward for its calories?
- [ ] Saturated fat low (a small share of the ~15 g/day cap)?
- [ ] Fats are unsaturated (olive oil / nuts / avocado / fish) and moderate per serving?
- [ ] No hard-constraint violations — grapefruit, alcohol, deep-fry, reflux triggers,
      fatty or cured meat, butter/cream? (§3)
- [ ] Reflux-safe flavoring — herbs / ginger / *cooked* alliums, not citrus / tomato
      / chili / raw garlic-onion?
- [ ] Reasonable fat load for one sitting (no gallbladder)?
- [ ] **Bonus, not required:** if it has starch, is it RS-friendly (cook-and-cool /
      raw oats)?

If the answer to the hard-constraint checks is clean and the recipe leans protein-
forward with good fats, it fits — regardless of whether it's a "complete" meal.
